Help & User Manual

🌟 About Hi Pi Network

Hi Pi Network is the first decentralized trust and reputation system built for the Pi Network ecosystem. Discover, Connect, and Trade with confidence.

Versionv1.0.0-BETA

🛠️ How It Works (For Pioneers)

1. Search: Find services by category, country, or city.

2. Vote: Rate providers from 1 to 5 stars. Your vote weight depends on your Tier level.

3. Quick Survey: Answer 4 short questions to help verify the provider's quality.

4. Report: If you see a fake or abusive review, tap the 🚩 Report button.

🏪 How It Works (For Providers)

1. Register: Fill the form and pay the 5 Pi fee.

2. Get Verified: Admin will review your documents.

3. Manage: Use the Merchant Console to reply to reviews and upgrade your subscription (Pro 15 Pi / Featured 25 Pi).

📜 Review Rules

1. Reviews must be based on real experiences.

2. No spam, offensive language, or external links.

3. No conflicts of interest (reviewing your own business).

⚖️ Dispute Resolution & Admin Authority

To ensure a fair ecosystem, if a review is reported as fake or abusive, the Admin will review the evidence (such as blockchain transaction history).

The Admin acts as the final arbiter to maintain community trust. Users and Providers agree to respect and comply with the Admin's final decision to avoid endless disputes. The Admin has the right to ban users or remove providers who violate the rules.

❓ FAQ

Q: How do I pay 5 Pi?
A: The app will automatically open your Pi Wallet when you click Register.

Q: Why was my review rejected?
A: It may have contained links, spam, or less than 10 characters.

Q: What is the ❓ icon?
A: Tap it next to any field to see a quick explanation.
