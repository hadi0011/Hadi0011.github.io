🌟 About Hi Pi Network

Hi Pi Network is the first decentralized trust and reputation system built for the Pi Network ecosystem. Discover, Connect, and Trade with confidence.

Versionv1.0.0-BETA

🛠️ How It Works

For Pioneers: Search for services, read reviews, answer Quick Surveys, and vote using your Pi account.

For Providers: Register your business, pay the 5 Pi fee, and get verified by admin to start receiving reviews.

📜 Review Rules

1. Reviews must be based on real experiences.

2. No spam, offensive language, or external links.

3. No conflicts of interest (reviewing your own business).

4. Emojis are allowed, but keep it professional.

❓ FAQ

Q: How do I pay 5 Pi?
