🌟 About PiTrustee

PiTrustee is the first decentralized trust and reputation system built for the Pi Network ecosystem.

Versionv1.0.0-BETA

🛠️ How It Works

For Pioneers: Search for services, read reviews, and vote using your Pi account.

For Providers: Register your business, pay 1 Pi, and get verified by admin.

📜 Review Rules

1. Reviews must be based on real experiences.

2. No spam, offensive language, or external links.

3. No conflicts of interest (reviewing your own business).

❓ FAQ

Q: How do I pay 1 Pi?
A: The app will automatically open your Pi Wallet when you click Register.

Q: Why was my review hidden?
A: It may have triggered our automated spam filter.
